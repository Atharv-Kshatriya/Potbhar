We reject the dangerous fallacy of allowing neural networks to certify biological safety. FreshGuard generates verifiable sensory evidence; certified food inspectors make the ultimate go/no-go determination.

policy

Strict Policy Notice: No "SAFE" badge from an ML score

An algorithm cannot smell rancidity or detect anaerobic toxins like *Clostridium botulinum*. Machine learning is used exclusively for anomaly detection, micro-texture degradation alerts, and logistical routing triage. Every edible batch requires physical inspector verification.
